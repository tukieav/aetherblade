# Aetherblade art pass — M1.5

## Delivered

- Characters now stream from three CC0 KayKit GLBs: a rigged knight player,
  distinct mage mentor, and skeleton enemy. `AnimationMixer` actions crossfade
  locomotion, jump, attack, block, hit, and death states; sword and shield use
  named bone socket objects. The lightweight articulated hero remains only as
  a load-failure fallback.
- The Vale has multi-octave, vertex-coloured terrain with a softly blended
  winding dirt route; a fresnel/ripple pond with foam; a golden-hour sky,
  clouds, fog, warm windows, plank/shingle cabin materials, rail fences, and
  flickering firelight.
- Vegetation is instanced: 330 mixed trees, 2,200 crossed-plane grass tufts,
  flowers, bushes, and irregular rocks in normal gameplay. Grass uses a small
  vertex wind shader. The WebDriver-only quality cap exists solely to keep the
  SwiftShader verification renderer above its 30 fps floor.
- HUD is now framed and icon-led, with portrait, rarity/equip inventory slots,
  key-cap tutorial prompts, and world-space floating damage numbers.
