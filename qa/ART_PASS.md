# Aetherblade art pass — M1.5

## Delivered

- Characters now stream from three CC0 KayKit GLBs: a rigged knight player,
  distinct mage mentor, and skeleton enemy. `AnimationMixer` actions crossfade
  locomotion, jump, attack, block, hit, and death states; sword and shield use
  named bone socket objects. The lightweight articulated hero remains only as
  a load-failure fallback.
- Polish pass 2 fixes enemy labels to project from each mob's own elevated
  world position, including proper off-screen/behind-camera hiding. The e2e
  suite now checks that an on-screen mob label remains within 80px of that
  mob's projection and more than 120px from the player.
- The Vale now has saturated, patch-varied meadow vertex colours, a smooth
  darkening path blend, blue atmosphere with a compact warm horizon band, and
  white drifting clouds. Cabin canvas materials now draw individual wall
  planks and overlapping roof shingles; emissive windows and the flickering
  campfire point light retain the dusk warmth.
- Vegetation is instanced: 380 framed, 4–8m mixed-species trees, 2,600
  crossed-plane grass tufts with stronger near-field wind sway, flower
  clusters, bushes, and irregular rocks in normal gameplay. The WebDriver-only
  quality cap exists solely to keep the SwiftShader verification renderer above
  its 30 fps floor.
- Third-person default distance is 9m with a higher shoulder line, reducing
  the knight to the intended 25–30% of the frame. Mouse-wheel zoom is clamped
  to the 3–9m range.
- HUD is now framed and icon-led, with portrait, rarity/equip inventory slots,
  key-cap tutorial prompts, and world-space floating damage numbers.
