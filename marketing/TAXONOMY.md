# Aetherblade — Category & Tags Rationale

## Category: Adventure (primary), Action (secondary)

**Adventure** is the recommended CrazyGames category:
- The core loop is exploration + quest progression (8-quest story chain across
  two maps), which is how CrazyGames' Adventure shelf is curated — story-driven
  3D worlds with combat as a means, not the whole game.
- Adventure on CrazyGames has less direct competition from hyper-polished
  action shooters, giving a low-poly ARPG a better ranking chance.
- Comparable successful titles (3D quest/combat RPGs) sit in Adventure.

**Action** is defensible — real-time sword combat, dodges, boss telegraphs —
but the Action shelf skews toward shooters/fighters with faster session loops;
Aetherblade's 20-40 min progression arc fits Adventure discovery better.

## Tags (8-10) and why

| Tag | Rationale |
|-----|-----------|
| 3D | High-traffic filter; the game is full WebGL 3D — table stakes for discovery. |
| RPG | Core identity: XP/levels, inventory, equipment, shop, quests. |
| Adventure | Mirrors the category; doubles discovery surface. |
| Action | Captures the real-time combat audience searching outside Adventure. |
| Fantasy | Setting descriptor players actively filter by; matches knight/meadow/cave art. |
| Sword | Weapon-specific searches ("sword games") convert well for melee ARPGs. |
| Monster | Slimes, wolves, golems, bosses — matches "monster" search intent. |
| Quest | Differentiator: an actual 8-quest chain, rare among portal games. |
| Loot | Signals the drop/equip reward loop to ARPG fans. |
| Mobile | The game has full touch controls — qualifies for mobile filters and homepage. |

10 tags total — maximizes search surface without diluting relevance (all ten
are true of the game; no aspirational tags that could trigger QA pushback).

## Audience & rating fit

Target 10-16, PEGI 12 aligned: stylized combat, no blood (particle dissolve),
no horror imagery even in the cave map — consistent with the bright,
saturated cover art required by the platform.
